==CPU scheduling is a process that allows one process to use the CPU while another is waiting.
It helps maximize CPU utilization and ensure fairness among processes.
There are several CPU scheduling algorithms: First-Come First-Served (FCFS), Shortest Job First (SJF), Round Robin (RR), and Priority Scheduling.
FCFS is simple but can cause convoy effect. SJF minimizes average waiting time but may cause starvation.
Round Robin gives time slices to each process and is commonly used in time-sharing systems.
Priority scheduling assigns priority; higher-priority jobs run first but low-priority jobs may starve.
